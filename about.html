About

The Baltic Beyblade Association (BBA) is an independent Beyblade community for players in Latvia, Lithuania and Estonia. This site lists tournaments, rankings and news for the region.

BBA is a fan community. It is not a national sports federation and is not affiliated with Hasbro or Takara Tomy.
