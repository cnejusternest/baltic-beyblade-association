About BBA

Independent coverage and competitive community for Beyblade across the Baltic states.

LATVIA · LITHUANIA · ESTONIA

Building the Baltic Beyblade scene.

The Baltic Beyblade Association (BBA) is an independent fan-run community hub for competitive Beyblade in Latvia, Lithuania and Estonia. We aim to make upcoming tournaments, players, rankings and results easier to follow.

This site is now a static information platform. Player accounts, submissions and live scores are not available. Tournament data, announcements and ranking lists are published after being added and verified by the site editors.

BBA is not a national sports federation and is not affiliated with Takara Tomy or Hasbro.
